__Control Your Computer with Just Your Fingers__

Say goodbye to your mouse! XD

With this program, your index finger becomes a magical mouse cursor! Just wave your hand in front of the webcam and watch the cursor follow your every move. It's like having an invisible mouse in the air

But wait, there's more... 

When you bring your index finger and thumb together, it's just like clicking the left mouse button! Pinch them together, and BAM – you've just clicked! 

___

 **How It Works ?**

 Show your hand → Your index finger becomes the mouse pointer

 Move your finger → The mouse glides smoothly across the screen

 Pinch index & thumb together → Left click! 

___

 **Installing Requirements**

 Before running the program, you need to install some Python libraries. Don't worry, it's super easy!

 
Now open your terminal or Command Prompt in the project folder and run:

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt

```

That's it!  Everything installs at once!

```bash
pip install opencv-python
```
